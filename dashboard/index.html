Model radar-2

Implied value follows only from the snapshot's own figures. No protocol name is special-cased; the same function scores every row.

implied = TVL × 0.30 × growth × maturity × farmability × breadth
growth     = 1 + clamp(change_7d, −60, +150) / 100 × 0.6
maturity   = 0.70 + 0.30 × min(age_days, 1095) / 1095   (age unknown: 365 days)
breadth    = 1 + 0.02 × min(chains − 1, 5)
ties are broken by $1, so rank 1 is always the most valuable protocol.

Farm plans

Every row carries six steps built from its own record — category, chain, TVL tier and age — each with what it costs and how long it takes: a fresh wallet, the one action to take, one qualifying action a week, a hold of 30–60 days, the points page check, and a farm log. Gas is priced by chain (Ethereum about $8, Solana about $0.05, L2s about $0.50, Bitcoin about $3). A row that cannot be farmed says so plainly and tells you what to watch instead of giving pretend steps.

Reading the board

“Records no token” means the protocol's DefiLlama record carries no token — it is not proof the protocol has none. The watchlist is the floor, never the ceiling: the board ranks all 342 pre-token protocols, and the 40 on the watchlist are marked with a star. Reference date is the snapshot's own ; the same snapshot always gives the same board.
